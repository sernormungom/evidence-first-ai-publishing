Multiply the illustrative daily amount by the eligible days. This is an example, not a company allowance.

## What does the calculation look like?

Assume two eligible days at {{example_daily_amount:unit}} each:

two × {{example_daily_amount:unit}} = **{{example_total_amount:unit}}**.
